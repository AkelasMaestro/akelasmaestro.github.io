I looked up, saw the mountains, the sunrise, and was hit with an awareness that my life is pretty good. In part because of the hussle and bussle, not in spite of it.

## The Mountains in Question

![[morning-commute 1.jpg|morning-commute 1.jpg]]

Taken at Provo Central Station,  a little after 7AM.

## The Interpretation

It had just snowed the night before, the first snow of the year. A welcome sight after last year's warm and dry summer. I was wearing my new puffy jacket and feeling cozy while waiting for my next bus with the other morning commuters.

The crisp air and white mountains led me to reminiscence about winter outings with the boy scouts. Good times for sure, but cold and uncomfortable. In that moment I much preferred my warm coat. 

Remembering afternoons spent waddling on snow shoes and evenings spent warming up numb toes made me appreciate the modern infrastructure around me. I included the UTA bus and the construction crane in the photo deliberately. I live a comfortable life thanks to these machines.

My wife and I came home from Game Weekend the day before. A long four day weekend of good food and good company concluded by flight cancellations which lost us a day. My wife and I on the back foot as we caught up on responsibilities. In such a moment, it would be easy to complain about going back to work and daydreaming of spending all our time doing what we wanted.

By the time I got on the bus, it was clear that I was doing what I wanted. I enjoy my classes and the puzzles they give me to solve. I enjoy commuting on public transit and the moments of waiting it gives me. I enjoy seeing and working with other people and feeling like I contribute something to society. My wife, the foodie that she is, was glad to return to work the moment a coworker brought in a cake to share.

Sometimes I get caught up in wanting a different life, and that line of thinking can lead to intense frustration. There is peace in being grateful and excitement in realizing I am exactly where I want to be. 

Be grateful where you can. Celebrate the little joys in your life, and look for the good that balances out the bad.
