Observations and comparisons between Brazilian and American finances.

## Earn in Dollar, Spend in Dollar

While I was visiting my in-laws in Brazil over the summer, one of the popular points of conversation I could participate in was money. Specifically in comparing lifestyles between [America](2026-05-04-all-american.md#) and Brazil. The median income in our respective countries is actually very similar when looking at the number amount in the bank. Things only get weird when you account for the exchange rate.
